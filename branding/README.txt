reSAID Lab — Logo & Brand Package
Responsible Software and AI Design Lab · Case Western Reserve University
================================================================

WHAT'S INSIDE
-------------
/vectors  — editable SVG source (scale to any size; edit in Illustrator,
            Figma, Inkscape, or any browser)
/raster   — ready-to-use PNGs (favicons, app/Slack icons, social card, slide)

Concept: the mark is a broken square whose two strokes cycle around a shared
center — the "re-" of reSAID: iteration, feedback, systems that reshape their
own inputs. The two copper terminals mark where the loop closes. In the
wordmark, the accent moves onto "AI" so the discipline reads at a glance.

TYPOGRAPHY
----------
Wordmark & headings   IBM Plex Mono  (SemiBold 600; "re-" in Regular 400)
Tagline & body        IBM Plex Sans  (SemiBold 600 kickers, Regular 400 body)

Both are free (SIL Open Font License): https://fonts.google.com/?query=IBM+Plex
The SVGs use live text — install IBM Plex Mono + IBM Plex Sans to edit it, or
use the PNGs where the font can't be installed.


CLEAR SPACE & MINIMUM SIZE
--------------------------
Clear space: keep a margin equal to the height of one copper terminal square
on every side of the lockup.
Minimum size: mark 16px; full lockup 120px wide. Below that, use the mark alone.


DO / DON'T
----------
DO   use navy or copper only; keep the "AI" accent; give the mark room.
DON'T recolor the mark outside the palette, stretch it, add gradients or
      shadows, rotate the loop, or box the lockup in.
